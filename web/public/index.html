IoT Attack Detection

An XGBoost model that sorts IoT network flows into 8 categories (Benign, DDoS, DoS, Mirai, Recon, Spoofing, BruteForce, Web). It was trained on a 5% sample of the CIC-IoT-2023 dataset. This is a research demo, not a security product.

What to keep in mind

  • The model relies strongly on three features (IAT, Number, Weight). Without them the 8-class macro-F1 falls from 0.83 to 0.67. I could not verify what IAT measures, so the real performance is probably somewhere between the two numbers.
  • Rare attacks (Web attacks, BruteForce) are recognised much worse than floods.
  • The data comes from one lab. Results on other networks may be different.
  • The test split is random, and neighbouring flows of a capture are similar, so the scores are probably somewhat optimistic.

Dataset: Neto, E. C. P., Dadkhah, S., Ferreira, R., Zohourian, A., Lu, R., Ghorbani, A. A. (2023). CICIoT2023: A real-time dataset and benchmark for large-scale attacks in IoT environment. Sensors, 23(13), 5941. doi.org/10.3390/s23135941
