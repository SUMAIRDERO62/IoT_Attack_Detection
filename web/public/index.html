01 / EVALUATION

Accuracy is not the whole story

Accuracy exceeded 0.99, while macro-F1 exposed weaker performance on less frequent attack families.

02 / FEATURE DEPENDENCE

Feature ablation matters

Removing IAT, Number and Weight reduced XGBoost 8-class macro-F1 from 0.832 to 0.668.

03 / GENERALIZATION

External validation is needed

Data comes from one lab environment; independent network captures are needed to test transferability.

Limitations & future work

Interpret results in the context of the experiment's known constraints.

Research transparency
Class imbalance and rare attacks

Web and BruteForce are recognized less reliably than high-volume flood categories.

Feature dependence

Performance falls when IAT, Number and Weight are excluded; their operational availability should be validated.

Dataset representativeness

The benchmark comes from one lab. Results may differ on other devices, topologies and traffic distributions.

Split and generalization

A random split may place similar neighboring flows across train and test sets, making results optimistic.
